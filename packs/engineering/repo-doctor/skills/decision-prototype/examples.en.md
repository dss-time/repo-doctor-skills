# Examples

- Logic: “Use a disposable prototype to decide whether the draft approval state model permits an invalid transition.” Define the exact invalid transition and verdict criterion, use in-memory synthetic data, and expose every relevant state.
- UI: “Before production work, compare two structurally different ways to present a dense settings flow.” Keep mutations stubbed and judge against declared usability criteria.
- Blocked: “Use our production database and customer credential so the prototype is realistic.” Do not connect; request an approved non-production substitute.
- Handoff: a supported result still routes to formal specification or implementation. Prototype files are not promoted unchanged.

Non-trigger: “Implement and ship the approved UI.” Use the appropriate specification, plan, and implementation workflow.
